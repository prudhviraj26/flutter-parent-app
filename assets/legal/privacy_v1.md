# Student & Parent Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 30, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Regulatory Compliance:** Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 & GDPR Compliant

At Veyho, safeguarding the privacy of students, parents, and educators is our utmost priority. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, process, protect, and dispose of personal data through the Veyho Parent Mobile Application and platform.

---

### 1. Data Fiduciary & Data Processor Relationship
- **Data Fiduciary:** Your child's educational institution ("School") acts as the primary Data Fiduciary determining the purpose and means of processing student and parent personal information.
- **Data Processor:** Veyho acts as the secure technical Data Processor under strict operational mandate from the School.

---

### 2. Categories of Information Collected
1. **Student Identifiers & Demographics:** Student full name, date of birth, gender, blood group, admission/GR number, APAAR ID, class, section, roll number, and enrolled academic year.
2. **Parent/Guardian Contact Data:** Father, mother, and guardian names, verified mobile numbers, residential addresses, and email addresses.
3. **Academic & Operational Data:** Daily attendance logs, leave applications, timetable assignments, homework circulars, examination marks, and fee payment history.
4. **Device Telemetry & Security Logs:** Hardware vendor identifier (Android ID / iOS IDFV), device model, operating system version, app build number, network IP address, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push tokens used for secure alert routing.
5. **No Invasive Hardware Tracking:** We strictly DO NOT collect IMEI numbers, hardware MAC addresses, telephonic call records, or persistent real-time GPS tracking when the application is in the background.

---

### 3. Purpose of Processing
We process personal and academic information exclusively to:
- Deliver real-time attendance alerts, school broadcasts, and emergency safety circulars.
- Facilitate direct teacher-parent communication regarding student performance and wellbeing.
- Provide secure online tuition fee receipts and breakdown summaries.
- Maintain tamper-proof legal consent records and prevent unauthorized multi-device account takeover.

---

### 4. Children's Data Protection & Consent
1. **Verifiable Parental Consent:** In accordance with the DPDP Act 2023, data relating to minors is processed strictly upon verifiable parental consent.
2. **Zero Behavioral Profiling:** We strictly do not profile students for behavioral advertising, commercial marketing, or sale to third-party data brokers.
3. **No Commercial Ads:** The Veyho Parent Mobile App contains zero third-party advertising or commercial sponsorships.

---

### 5. Data Security & Storage
1. **Encryption Standards:** All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3. Sensitive database records and credentials are cryptographically protected at rest using AES-256 encryption.
2. **Cloud Infrastructure:** Data is hosted in enterprise Azure data centers located in Central India, adhering to strict data localization mandates.
3. **Access Controls:** School staff access is strictly restricted through role-based access control (RBAC).

---

### 6. Retention & Right of Erasure
1. **Retention Schedule:** Student academic and attendance records are retained in compliance with the school's official statutory retention policy (typically 7 years following student graduation or transfer).
2. **Device Token Pruning:** When an application is uninstalled or a push token is invalidated by the mobile operating system, our backend automatically soft-deletes and prunes the inactive device record.
3. **Data Principal Rights:** Parents may request correction of erroneous records or review stored contact data through the school office or the designated school Data Protection Officer.
